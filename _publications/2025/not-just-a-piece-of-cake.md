abstract: >-
 We investigate cross-lingual fine-tuning for identifying idiomatic expressions, addressing the limited availability of annotated data across many languages. We evaluate encoder and generative decoder models to examine their ability to generalize idiom identification across languages. Additionally, we conduct an explainability study using linear probing and LogitLens to analyze how idiomatic meaning is represented across model layers. Results show consistent cross-lingual transfer, with English emerging as a strong source language. All code and models are released to support future research.

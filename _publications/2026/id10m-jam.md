abstract: >-
  Large language models achieve strong performance on idiom identification benchmarks, yet their robustness to misleading contextual signals remains largely untested. We introduce ID10M-JAM, an adversarial extension of ID10M that prepends conflicting context to target sentences: for each potential idiomatic expression, variants deliberately reverse contextual expectations, pairing literal cues with idiomatic usage and vice versa. All variants were validated by human annotators for naturalness and clarity. Our evaluation reveals systematic vulnerabilities in the contextual reasoning of current LLMs.

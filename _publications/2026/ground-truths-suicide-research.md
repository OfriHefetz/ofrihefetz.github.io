abstract: >-
  Recent advances in artificial intelligence and the availability of social media data have led to growing optimism about the ability to detect suicide risk at scale. Drawing on a recent umbrella review, we synthesize the current state of AI-based suicide detection in social media and examine the empirical foundations of this line of work, with particular attention to how ground truth labels are defined, obtained, and validated.

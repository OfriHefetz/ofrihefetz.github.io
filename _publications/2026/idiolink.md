abstract: >-
  Idioms pose a fundamental challenge for language models, as their meaning cannot be inferred from surface form alone. We introduce IdioLink, a retrieval benchmark designed to test whether models can link idiomatic expressions to conceptually equivalent meanings expressed in literal or paraphrased forms. IdioLink comprises 10,700 documents and 2,140 queries spanning 107 idioms with both literal and figurative uses, each annotated with spans that convey the core meaning. Evaluating strong embedding baselines (BGE, E5, Contriever, Qwen), we show that current models struggle to retrieve equivalent meanings across divergent surface realizations, relying instead on topical and shallow semantic cues.

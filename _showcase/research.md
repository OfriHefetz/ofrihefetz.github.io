## Mental Health and AI

My research focuses on developing computational methods for identifying and evaluating mental health conditions using multimodal signals, including speech, language, and behavioral data.

---
## Conversion Disorder  
**Collaboration with Ichilov Hospital**

This project explores the intersection of neurological and psychological conditions through computational analysis. We aim to identify patterns in language and behavior that may contribute to a better understanding and diagnosis of conversion disorder.

---

## Postpartum Depression (PPD)  
**Collaboration with Prof. Ruth Feldman**

In this project, we study behavioral and vocal markers of postpartum depression. The goal is to develop computational tools to assist in early detection and to provide objective measures of maternal mental health and interaction patterns.

---

## Suicide Risk Detection in Social Networks  
**Collaboration with Dr. Yaakov Ofir**

This research focuses on detecting suicidal ideation in social media platforms using natural language processing techniques. We explore how linguistic signals, expression patterns, and contextual cues can be used to identify at-risk individuals and support early intervention.

---


## PTSD and Audio Analysis  
**Collaboration with Dr. Yulia Golland and Prof. Rany Abend**

This project investigates the use of vocal and acoustic markers to analyze post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD). We aim to identify patterns in speech that reflect emotional and cognitive states, and to develop models that can support clinical assessment and treatment monitoring.

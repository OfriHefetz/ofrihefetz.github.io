Research Focus

My current research explores how vocal, linguistic, and behavioral signals can support the study of mental health conditions across several clinical contexts, including PTSD, postpartum depression, suicide risk detection, and conversion disorder.

I am especially interested in building an interpretable way that can complement traditional clinical assessment and help evaluate change over time.
